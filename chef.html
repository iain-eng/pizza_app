Capacity Mode

Total vs Per-Item Capacity

Total mode: each slot has one overall capacity limit — e.g. max 8 items across all orders in that slot.

Per-item mode: each menu item has its own limit per slot — e.g. max 2 Thai curries, max 4 loaded fries per slot. Items with no limit set are unlimited.
